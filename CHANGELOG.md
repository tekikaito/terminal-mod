# Changelog

## 0.3.1

The pane closes itself when the terminal has ended, instead of staying open with nothing to show.

## 0.3.0

The pane scrolls through the terminal's history (wheel, PageUp, PageDown) and pastes the clipboard
(Ctrl-V, Alt-V, Paste button).

## 0.2.0

The pane beside the chat shows colours and text attributes.

## 0.1.1

Smoother pane: an unchanged screen no longer redraws, typing shows at once, and an older screen can
no longer arrive after a newer one.

## 0.1.0

First release: `@term`, `/term`, `/term-pane`, the terminal pane, and the `terminal_read` and
`terminal_send` tools.
